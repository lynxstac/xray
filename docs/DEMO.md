# Demo

- The demo video is **English-only**. Voice: local Kokoro-82M TTS; music: Mixkit.
- **Short previews** live in `docs/`: [`demo.mp4`](demo.mp4) and [`demo.gif`](demo.gif).
- The **full-quality files** (YouTube 1080p/1440p, Reels/Shorts 1080×1920) are **not in git** (size). They are produced by the pipeline in `video/` (see [`../video/README.md`](../video/README.md)) and attached to GitHub Releases.
- **Reproduce the example** (nothing to download): `pnpm demo`.
- The screenshots in `docs/screenshots/` come from the English master recording of the open-source `taxonomy` repository.
